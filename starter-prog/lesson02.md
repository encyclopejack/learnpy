Have you ever wondered **if you have to print
*qwerty* 13 times?**

Yeah, you could just do

**

print("qwerty")

print("qwerty")

print("qwerty")

…

**

**But you wouldn’t do that, right?**

*We’re going to learn something called **variable** that helps
you do this.* 

So, a variable is like this:

*name = value*

now, for our “qwerty” example, we could do this:

**

x="qwerty"

print(x)

print(x)

print(x)

…

**

**THAT is wayyyyy more easier!**

Or, if you’re in an interpreter, you could do:

**

x="qwerty"

x

x

x

…

**
